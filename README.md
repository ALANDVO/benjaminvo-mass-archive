# Benjamin Vo — Daily Word archive

Public readings, reflections, prayers and recordings from [Benjamin’s memorial](https://benjaminvo.love/mass).

## Browse

- `readings/YYYY-MM-DD/LANGUAGE.json`: the prepared text, organized by date and language.
- [Audio releases](https://github.com/ALANDVO/benjaminvo-mass-archive/releases): original MP3 recordings, uploaded gradually and organized by date. Asset names include language, section and a content hash.
- Existing public pages remain at `https://benjaminvo.love/mass-readings/LANGUAGE/YYYY-MM-DD` so they retain their original addresses.

These services are a Catholic Liturgy of the Word and prayer in Benjamin’s memory, not a celebration of the Eucharist. Reflections and translations include AI-generated content. Uploaded recordings are preserved; the initial archive is still being populated. Readings retain their original attribution; no blanket license is granted over third-party material.

Website code, credentials and private submissions are excluded.
